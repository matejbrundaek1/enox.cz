Enter the Nyx

Dark fantasy, 15+

Current status: in development 

​

We follow the story of  gate keeper  Nikolas Fisher and nun Faustina who is actually a dryad  and also two brave children, Sophie and Peter. Last but not least, there is Albrech Zweistein, an alternate version of the famous scientist. All of them will have to undertake a dangerous journey to the realm of Nyx to prevent the penetration of the demonic forces of the hoodoo cult and the threat to humanity.

​

SHORT SYNOPSIS:

Nikolas Fisher unexpectedly "inherits" a madhouse for children.  The inheritance is a rather creepy institution in the mountains, in the vicinity of ancient ruins and mountain lakes. And some really strange things have been going on. At first, Nikolas spends his time unravelling these mysteries. Gradually, he finds out that: 

The whole place lies on the border of our world and a magical dimension, which the children from the asylum call Nyx.   Nicolas  falls in love with the nun - dryad Faustina, who helps him understand how things work here. 

Nicolas traces the conspiracy of the dark order of the Illuminati and the demonic forces on the other side of the gate. He joins forces with Albrecht Zweistein, the children of the Nyx Empire, and sets out to save the world alongside his dryad.

The young hero, Johannes Zach, begins our story as a skeptic. In order to convince his girlfriend of the true of his claims, he performs an evocation ritual with her, based on Faust's Grimoire. What follows both frightens him and makes him rethink his position on the dark sciences. 

Dark thriller  about love, power and soul loss.

Crackonosh vs Nazis  

Crazy comedy 12 +

​

A clever and sophisticated remix of the central European myth of mountain demon - Crakonosh. Czech magicians pretending to be botanists VS Hitler's mystical commando, pretending nothing at all VS English partisans in swing band disguise VS Crackonosh! The action comedy the world needed.

Crakonosh is a Polish, German and Czech myth. Ideal for a co-production project

 

The beginning of the Second World War in foothills area of North Sudetenland, where Wehrmacht soldiers are being recruited. That is where our treasure hunt begins. The treasure that originally belongs to legendary and mysterious demon of mountains The CRAKONOSH.

 

We follow the fates of three groups, each of them expects something different from the confrontation with the demon.

First and foremost, it is Hitler's commando searching the world for powerful artifacts. Crakonosh's magic wand is what they crave for. It will allow the Luftwaffe to control the weather.

Second is a Swing Band made up of British-trained guerrillas. They seek to obtain gold from the treasure to use it to support the anti-nazi resistance.

And last but not least, The Biologists - in fact a group of magicians from Charles University. Under the pretext of searching for a rare biotope known as Crakonosh's garden, they strive to evoke Rubezahl's/Krakonos ghost and turn it against the Hitler himself.

 

The paths of all groups will meet in small mountain hotel on the midsummer night when the rocks open to release their treasures. The magic brawl begins!

KEYWORDS: CRAZY COMEDY, WORLD WAR II, MAGIC, SWING

The Mage

Refer to a whole range of master works dedicated to food, such as classical short story “The Speciality of the House” and its “Amirstan Lamb”. Secret club restaurant that serves meat of its own customers. Also inspired by legendary gastro films such as “The Big Feast”, “The Cook, the Thief, His Wife and Her Lover”, "Suspiria”, or the series of Hannibal Lecter.

To this motive, we add the theme of a "secret society", an “elite club”, where it is possible to reveal deeper and stranger layers of how the club works. 

​

LOGLINE:

The base of the project is a detective story with a thriller atmosphere. Two women - twins. A lost supermodel, a former club member, and her twin sister: a detective who poses as her sister to infiltrate the club's structure and uncover its terrifying and wonderful secrets. But most of all, she tracks down where her sister has disappeared.
